# Contingency 2027

**What it is** — Contingency, **Wed 27 – Sun 31 January 2027**, Searles Leisure
Resort, Hunstanton. Wyrd Sisters Games Club, members-only, Warhorn booking. Tim GMs
**two games a day**, afternoon and evening, 4-hour slots — ten slots, eight booked,
both Sunday slots held as reserve.

**Where it lives** — `slaguru666/contingency2027`, mirrored under
`RPGS/Conventions/Contingency 2027`.

**Current state — the slate**

- Black Summer (TWDU, modified)
- [[afterimage]] (Blade Runner) — a **port** of the finished Continuum 2026 v2.2,
  not a rewrite
- **DEAD AIR I / II / III** (Call of Cthulhu: Berlin 1946 → a Soviet closed city
  1962 → Berlin 2027)
- The Favour (The Dee Sanction)
- Another Bloody Treasure Map (VANITY)
- The Curious Case of Mr Parker's Diary (Vaesen)

**Next steps**
- Check whether Witchfinder Garrett is genuinely *returning* — he is described that
  way but appears nowhere in earlier convention material
- Replace the placeholder slot times once Warhorn publishes

**Key decisions**
- **DEAD AIR is one production with three outputs**, not three scenarios. That is
  what makes eight games deliverable in the time available.
- It runs the same afternoon slot on three consecutive days, so a player can take
  the whole arc without a clash.
- AFTERIMAGE is ported, not rewritten — the Continuum version is finished work.
