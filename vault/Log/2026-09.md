## 2026-09-13 — the standalone Corkboard runs

Slice one of the standalone app is built and verified live: a browser page with
no Foundry that creates, names, opens and deletes boards; adds cards and zones;
drags, resizes, ties string, draws and rubs out; and reopens a board with
everything still there. Not a second corkboard — the renderer, the stylesheet,
every gesture and all the arithmetic are the module's own files, reached through
the `BoardHost` seam. 781 tests. Head `5246a85`, pushed. Record in
`docs/verification/2026-09-13-standalone-slice-1.md`.

- **The multi-tab lock is `navigator.locks`, not a `BroadcastChannel` election.**
  Contracts §5 amended in place. A BroadcastChannel post from `pagehide` is
  queued as a task and the document is destroyed before it runs, so closing the
  tab holding a board left the other read-only for good, and nothing in an
  announce-and-listen protocol can hear a tab that died without speaking. The
  browser owns its lock and releases it when the tab goes. Every decision in §5
  stands; only the mechanism moved.
- **A change is shown only after it is stored.** Showing first is faster by one
  IndexedDB round trip and lies whenever the write fails — which on a device
  whose storage can be evicted is the expensive option.
- **Writes are declared as operation lists rather than handed-out transactions**,
  which makes "the board and its commit land together" structural and sidesteps
  a transaction held across an `await` being closed by the browser.
- **The gesture arbitration order lives in one place** now that there are two
  hosts. Two literals agree only while somebody remembers they must.
- Live verification found six defects. Five were mine and are fixed. The sixth —
  a pen tap storing a one-point stroke that can never be seen, selected or
  removed — is in shared code, is in the module today, and needs its own
  decision, so it is recorded and spawned as its own task.
