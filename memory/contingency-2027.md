Contingency 2027 runs **Wed 27 – Sun 31 January 2027** at Searles Leisure Resort,
Hunstanton (Wyrd Sisters Games Club, members-only, Warhorn booking). Tim GMs
**two games a day — afternoon and evening, 4-hour slots** = ten slots; eight
booked, both Sunday slots held as reserve.

Repo: `slaguru666/contingency2027`. Mirror:
`~/Library/Mobile Documents/com~apple~CloudDocs/RPGS/Conventions/Contingency 2027`.

The slate: Black Summer (TWDU, modified) · AFTERIMAGE (Blade Runner — a port of
the finished Continuum 2026 v2.2, *not* a rewrite) · **DEAD AIR** I/II/III (Call
of Cthulhu: Berlin 1946 → Soviet closed city 1962 → Berlin 2027) · The Favour
(Dee Sanction) · Another Bloody Treasure Map (VANITY) · The Curious Case of
Mr Parker's Diary (Vaesen).

DEAD AIR runs the same afternoon slot on three consecutive days so a player can
take the whole arc without a clash, and is produced as **one production with
three outputs** — that is what makes eight games deliverable. See
[[rpg-skill]] for the house format the repo is written to.

Two things to check before drafting further: Witchfinder Garrett is described as
*returning* but appears nowhere in earlier convention material, and the slot
times in the repo are placeholders until Warhorn publishes.
